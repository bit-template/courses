## 1. Summarize the Course

 This course teaches beginners hoe to write a research paper from the start to finish. It explains how to choose and plan a research topic,write the introduction and literature review,develop research questions,describe the methodology, present results, discuss findings, and write the abstract and conclusion.This course also covers proper paraphrasing, referencing, citation, and avoiding plagiarism.Overall,it provides a clear understanding of the structure and process of writing an effective acadamic research paper.

---

## 2. Key Learnings

    • Learned the  basic structure of a research paper.
    • Understood how to choose a research topic and research questions.
    • Learned to write the introduction, literature, review, methodology, results, and conclusion.
    • Learned proper paraphrasing, referencing and citation.
    • Understood how to avoid plagiarism.
    • Learned how to present research finding in a clear and academic manner.


---

## 3. Practical Application

        The knowledge can be applied to prepare academic research papers, project reports, and technical papers by selecting a suitable topic, conducting a literature review, presenting research findings, and properly citing sources while avoiding plagiarism.


---

## 4. Real-World Example

  A real-world example is a B.Tech IT student writing a research paper on “AI-based Student Attendance System.” Using this course, the student can identify the research problem, review previous studies, write the methodology used to develop the system, present the results, discuss the findings, and prepare the conclusion and references. The course uses many examples and explains complete research papers to show how these sections fit together.

---

## 5. Limitations
The Limitations, Challenges, or Situations Where These Concepts Should Not Be Used concepts are not necessary for casual writing, personal messages, simple assignments, or everyday communication. Writing a research paper can also be time-consuming and requires reliable sources, proper data, and accurate analysis. Therefore, the concepts should not be applied when formal research, academic evidence, and detailed referencing are not required.
